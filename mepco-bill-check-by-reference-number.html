MEPCO Bill Check by Reference Number

Your MEPCO reference number is the key to checking your bill online. It is a 14-digit number, and you enter it on the official portal — bill.pitc.com.pk/mepcobill (MEPCO Web Bill). No login, no OTP, no CNIC is needed. The portal only asks for your reference number or your Customer ID.

Quick check: Go to the official MEPCO Web Bill page, enter your 14-digit reference number, pick U (Urban) or R (Rural) as printed on your bill, and search. Your current bill — amount, due date and units used — will show on the screen.

Where to find your reference number

On a printed MEPCO bill, the reference number is at the top-left, above the barcode, labelled "Ref No". Keep an old bill safe — the reference number stays the same month after month, so one old bill is enough for every future check.

The 14 digits are made up of three parts: 2 digits (batch) + 5 digits (sub-division) + 7 digits (reference). You do not need to split them yourself — just type all 14 digits together, exactly as printed, with no spaces or dashes.

U or R — which one do I pick?

Next to the reference-number box, the portal asks you to choose U (Urban) or R (Rural). Pick the one printed on your bill next to your reference number. If you pick the wrong one, the portal will not find your bill — that does not mean your bill does not exist, it just means the U/R choice does not match your connection. Try the other option before worrying.

If a website or app asks for your meter number or CNIC to "check your bill", be careful: the official portal does not work that way. It asks only for the reference number or Customer ID.

Steps: check your bill by reference number

  1. Take your (old or new) MEPCO bill and find the 14-digit reference number at the top-left, above the barcode.
  2. Open the official portal: bill.pitc.com.pk/mepcobill.
  3. Enter the 14 digits exactly as printed — no spaces, no dashes.
  4. Select U or R exactly as shown on your bill.
  5. Search. Your bill will appear with the amount payable, due date, units consumed and meter readings.
  6. To keep a copy, use your browser's Print option and choose "Save as PDF" — see our duplicate bill download guide.

"Given Ref/App No is invalid" — what does it mean?

This is the exact error message the official portal shows when it cannot match what you typed. It usually means one of these:

  • A typing mistake — one wrong digit is enough. Re-type the number slowly from the bill, digit by digit.
  • Wrong U/R choice — try the other option (U instead of R, or R instead of U).
  • You typed the wrong number — for example the meter number or an old/wrong bill's number. Use the 14-digit reference number from the top-left of the bill, or the 10-digit Customer ID instead.
  • Bill not generated yet — if the new month's bill has not been made yet, the portal may not find it. Check again after a day or two, or check the previous month's bill to confirm your number works.

If the number is typed correctly, the U/R matches your bill, and the error still appears, call the MEPCO helpline 118 or visit your nearest Customer Service Centre with your bill and CNIC.

Tip: New bill not showing yet? Bills are generated at different times in the month for different areas. If last month's bill opens fine with the same number, your number is correct — the new bill simply is not ready yet.

What you will see after a successful check

Your bill on screen shows the amount to pay, the due date, the units used this month, and the current and previous meter readings. Compare the readings with your meter at home — if they do not match, read our guide on understanding your bill charges and, if needed, how to complain.

Frequently asked questions

Where is the MEPCO reference number on the bill?

Top-left, above the barcode, labelled "Ref No". It is 14 digits long.

Can I check my MEPCO bill with my Customer ID instead?

Yes. The official portal accepts either the 14-digit reference number (with U/R) or the 10-digit Customer ID.

What is the difference between U and R?

U means Urban and R means Rural. Pick the one printed on your bill next to your reference number — the wrong choice will not find your bill.

Can I check my bill with my meter number or CNIC?

No. The official portal has no meter-number lookup and no CNIC lookup. Only the reference number or Customer ID works online.

What does "Given Ref/App No is invalid" mean?

It means the portal could not match what you entered. Usually a typing mistake, the wrong U/R choice, the wrong number typed, or a bill that has not been generated yet.

Is checking my bill on the official portal free?

Yes — free, with no login, no OTP and no fee.
