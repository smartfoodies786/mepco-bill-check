MEPCO Bill Payment

You can pay your MEPCO bill in many ways — from your phone in a minute, or in person at a bank or post office. In every case, payment is made against your 14-digit reference number (or Customer ID where the app asks for it). You only need the bill itself to get that number — check it on the official portal (bill.pitc.com.pk/mepcobill) if the paper bill has not arrived.

Late payment — 10% surcharge

If you pay after the due date, a late payment surcharge (LPS) of 10% is added to your bill. Pay on or before the due date to avoid it. And remember: if your paper bill never arrived, the due date does not extend — check your bill online and pay by the printed due date (see duplicate bill download).

Due-date extension and installments

If you cannot pay the full bill by the due date, ask MEPCO about a due-date extension or paying in installments — do this before the due date, at your Customer Service Centre or SDO office, not after the bill is already late.

Guides that cite the NEPRA consumer manual say there is no markup if the first installment is paid by the due date, and that a markup applies after that — per NEPRA consumer manual as cited by guides, confirm with MEPCO.

Whatever arrangement you agree, get it in writing from the MEPCO office, note the installment dates, and pay every installment on time — a missed installment can cancel the arrangement.

Keep your receipt for 6 months

After every payment — online or at a counter — keep the receipt or confirmation for at least 6 months. If a payment is ever shown as unpaid on a later bill, the receipt is your proof. For mobile payments, save the confirmation SMS and take a screenshot of the success screen; for counter payments, keep the stamped slip.

MEPCO SMART app

MEPCO's SMART app is MEPCO's own mobile app for consumer services (bill viewing and related services). Download it only from the official app store (Google Play / App Store) — never from a link sent by a stranger or a random website — and never share your reference number, CNIC or OTP with anyone claiming to be from the app's support.

Tip: After paying online, wait a day and re-check your bill on the official portal to confirm the payment has been recorded. If it still shows unpaid after a few days, contact your bank/wallet first with your receipt, then MEPCO on 118.

Frequently asked questions

How do I pay my MEPCO bill from JazzCash or Easypaisa?

Open the app → Bill Payment → Electricity → MEPCO → enter your 14-digit reference number → confirm the amount and pay.

What happens if I pay after the due date?

A late payment surcharge of 10% is added to your bill.

Can I pay my MEPCO bill in installments?

Yes, in many cases — but arrange it with MEPCO before the due date, at your Customer Service Centre or SDO office, and get the arrangement in writing.

How long should I keep my payment receipt?

At least 6 months — it is your proof if a payment is ever shown as unpaid later.

I paid online but the bill still shows unpaid. What do I do?

Wait a day for the payment to be recorded, then re-check on the official portal. If it still shows unpaid, contact your bank or wallet with your receipt, then MEPCO on 118.

Can I pay from abroad?

Yes — overseas Pakistanis can pay through a Roshan Digital Account (RDA) bill-payment option using the same reference number.
