Check Your MEPCO Bill in 60 Seconds — 4 Steps

  1. Find your reference number. It is the 14-digit number at the top-left of your bill, above the barcode, labelled “Ref No”. Any old bill has it too.
  2. Check U or R. Look at the letter printed next to your reference number: U means Urban, R means Rural. Pick the same one in the box above — the wrong letter can show “no record”.
  3. Press “Check Bill”. The official MEPCO Web Bill page (PITC portal) opens in a new tab. Type the same reference number there and press submit.
  4. View, download, or print. Your current bill appears on screen. Use your browser’s print option to print it or save it as a PDF — that is your duplicate bill.

No account, no password, no OTP, and no CNIC are needed for the online check. If you only have your 10-digit Customer ID (also printed on the bill), the portal accepts that instead of the reference number.

Where Is the Reference Number on a MEPCO Bill?

Look at the top-left corner of your bill, just above the barcode. The number is labelled “Ref No” and has 14 digits. The U/R letter sits next to it. Keep one old bill safe at home — its reference number never changes, so you can use it every month.

Protected vs Unprotected Consumers — In Short

Your per-unit rate depends on whether you are a protected or unprotected consumer. Protected consumers pay lower slab rates.

Consumer typeRuleWhat it means for your bill
ProtectedUsed 200 units or less per month for the previous 6 months in a rowLower per-unit slab rates
UnprotectedUsed more than 200 units in any of the previous 6 monthsHigher per-unit slab rates

Crossing 200 units in even one month can move you to unprotected rates, which is one of the commonest reasons a bill suddenly jumps. Understand your MEPCO bill charges, slabs, FPA, and taxes.

Your MEPCO Bill Cycle — Month by Month

MEPCO bills run on a monthly cycle. Every month follows the same four steps — the dates are printed on your bill, and your reference number never changes, so the check is the same every month.

StepWhat happensWhat you do
1. Meter readingYour meter is read for the monthNote your own reading — a dated photo helps if the bill reading is wrong
2. Bill issuedYour monthly bill is prepared and deliveredIf the paper bill has not arrived, check online on the portal
3. Due dateThe last date to pay without a surcharge, printed on the billPay before this date — a late paper bill does not move the due date
4. PayPay at a bank, ATM, JazzCash, Easypaisa or post officeKeep your receipt for at least 6 months

Exact reading and due dates differ by area and month — they are printed on your own bill. This site does not publish fixed dates, because they change every cycle.

How to Pay Your MEPCO Bill

Pay through JazzCash, Easypaisa, any 1LINK bank app, an ATM, a bank counter, or a post office. Keep your receipt for at least 6 months. Paying after the due date adds a late payment surcharge of 10%.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I check my MEPCO bill online?

Enter your 14-digit reference number in the checker box at the top of this page, choose U (Urban) or R (Rural) as printed on your bill, and press Check Bill. The official MEPCO Web Bill page on the PITC portal (bill.pitc.com.pk/mepcobill) opens in a new tab, where you enter the same details to view your bill. There is no login, no fee, and this site does not fetch or store your bill.

Where is my MEPCO reference number on the bill?

Your 14-digit reference number is printed at the top-left of your paper bill, above the barcode, labelled “Ref No”. It is also printed on any previous bill, so an old bill works for the online check too.

What is U/R on the MEPCO bill check form?

U stands for Urban and R stands for Rural. Pick the letter printed next to the reference number on your bill. Choosing the wrong one can show no record, even if the number is correct.

Can I check my MEPCO bill with my Customer ID?

Yes. The official PITC portal accepts either your 14-digit reference number (with the U/R selector) or your 10-digit Customer ID. The Customer ID is printed on your bill. There is no meter-number lookup and no CNIC lookup on the portal.

Can I check my MEPCO bill by CNIC number?

No. The official online portal does not offer a CNIC lookup. If you have lost your bill and do not know your reference number or Customer ID, you can recover it from an old bill, by calling helpline 118, or by visiting your nearest MEPCO customer service centre with your original CNIC.

Is a duplicate MEPCO bill valid at banks?

Yes. A duplicate bill downloaded or printed from the official PITC portal is the same bill as the one delivered to your home, and you can pay it at any bank counter, ATM, or through JazzCash, Easypaisa, or your bank’s app. A missing paper bill does not extend your due date, so check online and pay on time.

How can I pay my MEPCO bill online?

You can pay through JazzCash, Easypaisa, any 1LINK bank app, an ATM, a bank counter, or a post office. Keep your payment receipt for at least 6 months. Paying after the due date adds a late payment surcharge of 10%.

What is the late payment surcharge on a MEPCO bill?

A late payment surcharge of 10% is added if you pay after the due date printed on your bill. The due date does not move just because the paper bill arrived late or never arrived — check your bill online and pay before the due date.

Why is my MEPCO bill higher than usual?

The usual reasons are more units used, moving into a higher tariff slab, a fuel price adjustment (FPA) added to the bill, or a change from protected to unprotected status. Compare the units on this month’s bill with last month’s, and check your meter reading against the reading printed on the bill.

Who do I call for a wrong MEPCO bill?

Call the national electricity helpline 118 or MEPCO’s toll-free number 0800-63726. You can also lodge a complaint on the PITC complaint portal at ccms.pitc.com.pk or email ccms@pitc.com.pk. Keep your bill and a dated photo of your meter reading ready before you call.
