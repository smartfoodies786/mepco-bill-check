MEPCO Duplicate Bill Download / Print

A "duplicate bill" is simply another copy of the same bill. There is no separate duplicate-bill system: a duplicate is the same lookup on the official portal — bill.pitc.com.pk/mepcobill — using your 14-digit reference number (with U/R) or your 10-digit Customer ID. The bill you see on screen is your duplicate bill, ready to print or save.

In short: Open the official MEPCO Web Bill page, enter your reference number, and print the bill that appears. That printout is your duplicate bill — no separate form, no fee on the portal, no login.

How to download / print your duplicate bill

  1. Open the official portal: bill.pitc.com.pk/mepcobill.
  2. Enter your 14-digit reference number and select U or R as printed on an old bill — or enter your 10-digit Customer ID. (Lost your number? See bill without reference number.)
  3. When your bill appears, check the amount, due date and readings.
  4. To save a copy: use your browser's Print option (Ctrl+P / Cmd+P) and choose "Save as PDF" as the printer. Save it on your phone or computer.
  5. To print on paper: in the same Print window, choose your printer instead of "Save as PDF". Print the full page so the reference number, barcode area, amount and due date are all visible.

Is a printed duplicate bill valid at banks?

Yes. A duplicate bill printed from the official portal is accepted for payment at banks and other payment points, because it carries the same reference number, amount and due date as the original bill. The payment counter reads the reference number — that is what matters, not whether the paper came through the post. Keep the printout clean and complete, with the reference number and amount clearly readable, and keep your payment receipt afterwards.

Printing at a shop

If you have no printer at home, any photocopy / computer shop can print it for you. Either:

  • Open the portal on your phone, show the bill on screen, and ask the shop to print it; or
  • Save the bill as a PDF on your phone first, then send the PDF to the shop (Bluetooth, WhatsApp or USB) and ask for a print.

Do not hand your phone unlocked to a stranger, and do not let a shop save or share your bill. Your bill shows your name, address and reference number — ask the shop to delete the file from their computer after printing.

Duplicate bill from a Customer Service Centre (CSC)

You can also get a duplicate bill free of charge from your nearest MEPCO Customer Service Centre, the same day. Take your CNIC and, if you have it, any old bill. The staff can look up your connection and print a duplicate for you. This is the best option if you have lost your reference number entirely — the centre can find your connection with your CNIC and property details (see bill without reference number).

Important — the due date does NOT extend: If your bill never arrived at your home, your due date stays the same. "Bill nahi mila" is not accepted as a reason for late payment, and the 10% late payment surcharge still applies after the due date. Check your bill online every month — do not wait for the paper bill.

When the duplicate bill does not show

  • "Given Ref/App No is invalid" — re-check your digits and your U/R choice; try your Customer ID instead. Full detail: check by reference number.
  • Bill not generated yet — the new month's bill may not be ready. Try again after a day or two.
  • Old bill shows instead of the new one — make sure you are looking at the latest bill on the portal and not a PDF you saved last month.

Frequently asked questions

How do I download a MEPCO duplicate bill?

Look up your bill on the official portal (bill.pitc.com.pk/mepcobill) with your reference number or Customer ID, then use Print → "Save as PDF". That file is your duplicate bill.

Is a duplicate bill the same as the original bill?

Yes — same reference number, same amount, same due date. It is simply another copy of the same bill.

Can I pay with a printed duplicate bill at a bank?

Yes. Banks accept it because payment is made against the reference number on the bill.

Can I get a duplicate bill free from a MEPCO office?

Yes — a Customer Service Centre gives a duplicate free, the same day. Take your CNIC and any old bill if you have one.

My bill never arrived. Does my due date move?

No. The due date does not extend if the bill never arrived. Check online and pay by the printed due date to avoid the 10% late surcharge.

Do I need to log in or pay to get a duplicate bill?

No. The official portal needs no login, no OTP and no fee.
