MEPCO's job or your electrician's job?

The rule is simple: everything from the pole to your meter belongs to MEPCO. Everything after the meter, inside your home, is a private electrician's job.

  • MEPCO's job: meter faults, no supply to the whole street or area, pole and transformer faults, and meter reading or billing problems. Call helpline 118 or toll-free 0800-63726.
  • Your electrician's job: house wiring, switches and sockets, fans and lights, breaker (MCB) replacement, UPS and solar wiring, and earthing.

If you are not sure which side the fault is on, call 118 first — MEPCO can tell you whether it is a supply fault before you pay anyone.

Safety first — before any electrical work

  • Switch off the main breaker before any work starts, and keep it off until the work is done.
  • Never touch live wires, and never work on wiring with wet hands or while standing in water.
  • Keep water away from sockets, switches and the breaker box.
  • If you smell burning, see sparking, or the breaker keeps tripping, stop and call a professional — do not keep resetting the breaker.
  • Keep children away from the work area while wiring is open.

How to choose a good electrician in Multan

  1. Ask how much experience they have with the job you need — house wiring, fans, breakers and solar wiring are different skills.
  2. Get two quotes and compare what each one includes — labour, material and fitting should be clear in both.
  3. Agree the price in writing before work starts, and agree what happens if extra work is found mid-job.
  4. Ask about warranty on the work — a good electrician stands behind their fitting and wiring.
  5. Check their ID and note their name and number before they start.

Common electrical jobs at home

  • New house wiring, and rewiring of old wiring
  • Fan and light fitting, including ceiling fans and LED lights
  • Breaker (MCB) replacement and distribution board work
  • UPS wiring and battery connections
  • Solar panel wiring and inverter connections
  • Earthing for the home, especially before solar or UPS work
