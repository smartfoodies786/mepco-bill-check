MEPCO Bill Online Check Muzaffargarh

MEPCO supplies electricity in Muzaffargarh district, South Punjab. Checking your bill online works the same way in every MEPCO district — there is no separate Muzaffargarh portal. Use your 14-digit reference number (with U/R) or your 10-digit Customer ID on the official PITC portal.

Official bill page: bill.pitc.com.pk/mepcobill — “MEPCO Web Bill”. Enter your 14-digit reference number, choose U (Urban) or R (Rural) as printed on your bill, and press submit. No login, no fee, no CNIC needed. This site never fetches or stores your number.

How to check your MEPCO bill in Muzaffargarh — 4 steps

  1. Find your 14-digit reference number — top-left of any old or new bill, above the barcode, labelled “Ref No”. Any old bill works; the number never changes.
  2. Check the U/R letter next to it: U means Urban, R means Rural. Pick the same one on the portal — the wrong letter can show “no record”.
  3. Open the official portal — bill.pitc.com.pk/mepcobill — and enter the same details.
  4. View, download or print your bill. The printout or PDF is your duplicate bill and can be paid at any bank.

Quick answers for Muzaffargarh

  • Helplines: 118 (national electricity helpline) and MEPCO toll-free 0800-63726.
  • SMS check: Text PITC, a space, then your 14-digit reference number to 8334 for bill amount and due date. 8118 is for complaints and alerts — not the bill check. See SMS check.
  • Pay the bill: JazzCash, Easypaisa, any 1LINK bank app, ATM, bank counter or post office. Pay before the due date — late payment adds a 10% surcharge. See payment guide.
  • Wrong bill or complaint: call 118 or 0800-63726, or lodge a complaint on ccms.pitc.com.pk. See complaint / helpline.
  • Duplicate bill: print or save-as-PDF from the official portal — it is the same bill and valid at banks. See duplicate bill download.

Frequently asked questions — Muzaffargarh

Can I check my MEPCO bill online in Muzaffargarh?

Yes. Use the official PITC portal at bill.pitc.com.pk/mepcobill with your 14-digit reference number (U/R) or 10-digit Customer ID. The process is the same in every MEPCO district, including Muzaffargarh.

What is the MEPCO helpline for Muzaffargarh?

Call the national electricity helpline 118 or MEPCO toll-free 0800-63726. There is no separate district helpline number — these two numbers cover all 13 MEPCO districts.

Can I check my MEPCO bill by SMS in Muzaffargarh?

Yes. Text PITC, a space, then your 14-digit reference number to 8334. The reply gives your bill amount and due date. 8118 is for complaints and alerts, not the bill check.
