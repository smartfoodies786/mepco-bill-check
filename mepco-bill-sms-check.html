MEPCO Bill SMS Check

There is no smartphone or internet at hand for everyone — so MEPCO bills can also be checked by SMS. But the SMS numbers are widely mixed up online, so here is the honest version: which number does what, the exact format, and what to do when there is no reply.

Bill by SMS: Text PITC, a space, then your 14-digit reference number — to 8334. Example: PITC 12345678901234 (use your own reference number). The reply gives your bill amount and due date.

The two SMS numbers — do not mix them up

NumberWhat it is for
8334Bill check by SMS. Send PITC + space + your 14-digit reference number. Reply: bill amount and due date.
8118Complaints and alerts. This is the complaints/alerts SMS short code — not the bill-check number.

Many guides online give the wrong number for the bill check, or present 8118 as the bill-check SMS. That is the confusion this page corrects: 8334 for the bill, 8118 for complaints/alerts.

Steps: check your bill by SMS

  1. Find your 14-digit reference number on an old bill (top-left, above the barcode) — see check by reference number.
  2. Open a new SMS on your phone.
  3. Type PITC, then a space, then your 14-digit reference number — no dashes, no extra words.
  4. Send it to 8334.
  5. Wait for the reply SMS, which gives your bill amount and due date.

Bijli Bhai says: SMS se bill check karna sab se asaan hai jab net na ho — PITC, space, phir apna 14-digit number, 8334 par bhej do. Bas number sahi likhna, aik digit ghalat hua to jawab nahi aayega.

Charges

Standard SMS charges apply — the SMS to 8334 is charged at your mobile network's normal SMS rate. The reply is a normal incoming SMS. Check your package/rate with your mobile operator if you are unsure.

No reply? Do this

  • Check the format first: PITC in capital letters, one space, then exactly 14 digits, no dashes. One wrong digit, or the wrong number sent, means no useful reply.
  • Check your balance: if you have no SMS balance or no package, the message may not go through.
  • Wait a few minutes and try once more. Network delays happen.
  • Use the portal instead: if there is still no reply, check your bill on the official portal — bill.pitc.com.pk/mepcobill — with the same reference number, or call the helpline 118. The SMS method is a convenience; the portal is the reliable check, and it shows the full bill, not just the amount and due date.

Tip: The SMS reply gives only the amount and due date. For the full bill — units used, readings, FPA/QTA and taxes — use the portal and save/print the bill (see duplicate bill download).

Frequently asked questions

What is the SMS format to check my MEPCO bill?

Send PITC, a space, and your 14-digit reference number to 8334. Example: PITC 12345678901234 (with your own number).

What does the SMS reply tell me?

Your bill amount and due date. For the full bill details, use the official portal.

Is 8118 the MEPCO bill-check number?

No. 8118 is for complaints and alerts. The bill-check number is 8334.

Does the SMS check cost anything?

Standard SMS charges apply, at your mobile network's normal rate.

I sent the SMS but got no reply. What now?

Re-check the format and your 14 digits, check your SMS balance, wait a few minutes and try once more. If there is still no reply, check on the official portal or call 118.

Can I check my bill by SMS using my CNIC or meter number?

No. The SMS check needs your 14-digit reference number. There is no CNIC or meter-number lookup.
