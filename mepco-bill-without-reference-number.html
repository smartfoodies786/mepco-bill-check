MEPCO Bill Without Reference Number

Honest answer first: There is no CNIC lookup on the official portal. The official MEPCO Web Bill page (bill.pitc.com.pk/mepcobill) asks only for a 14-digit reference number or a 10-digit Customer ID — no login, no OTP, no CNIC field. Some websites wrongly claim you can retrieve your bill by CNIC online. That is not true. Only a MEPCO office can find your connection using your CNIC. Do not type your CNIC into any third-party "bill check by CNIC" website or app.

If you have lost your reference number, work through these recovery routes in order — start at the top, and only move down the list if the step above does not work.

Recovery routes, in order

  1. Find any old bill — use its Customer ID. The reference number stays the same every month, and so does the 10-digit Customer ID printed on the bill. Any old MEPCO bill — even a year old — has both numbers. Enter the Customer ID on the official portal and your current bill will appear.
  2. Ask family for a photo of the bill. If a family member has the bill, or an old bill photo on their phone, ask them to send a clear photo of the top of the bill, where the reference number is printed. One photo is enough — the number never changes.
  3. Call the helpline 118. Call 118 and explain that you have lost your reference number. Have your CNIC, your name, and your address ready. The helpline can guide you on how your connection can be traced.
  4. Visit your Customer Service Centre (CSC) or SDO office with your CNIC and a property document. This is the sure route. Take your original CNIC and a document that proves the property/connection is yours (for example, a property document or a previous bill in your name). The office staff can look up your connection by CNIC and give you your reference number — and a duplicate bill, free, the same day.
  5. SMS 8118. 8118 is MEPCO's complaints/alerts SMS short code. Some guides suggest an SMS route for finding your reference number, but this is not confirmed — do not rely on it as your main route. Use the office visit in step 4 if the steps above have not worked.

Once you have your number again

Write the 14-digit reference number (and the U/R letter) and the 10-digit Customer ID somewhere safe — on your phone, in a notebook, or on a photo of the bill saved in your gallery. Next month you will not need to search again. Also save the bill as a PDF when you check it online, so you always have a copy — see duplicate bill download.

Tip: If you rent the house or shop, ask the owner or the previous tenant for an old bill photo. The reference number belongs to the connection (the meter), not to a person, so it stays the same even when the occupant changes.

Warnings

  • Never pay anyone to "find" your reference number. MEPCO offices provide this free. Anyone asking for money for it is not acting for MEPCO.
  • Never share your CNIC photo with a random website, agent or WhatsApp number claiming to check your bill. Use only the official portal or a MEPCO office in person.
  • Do not trust "bill check by CNIC" apps. There is no official CNIC bill-check service online. Such apps may misuse your CNIC details.

Frequently asked questions

Can I check my MEPCO bill by CNIC online?

No. The official portal has no CNIC lookup. Only a MEPCO office can trace your connection using your CNIC.

I have no old bill at all. What do I do?

Visit your nearest Customer Service Centre or SDO office with your original CNIC and a property document. Staff can find your connection and give you your reference number and a duplicate bill, free, the same day.

Can I use the Customer ID instead of the reference number?

Yes — the 10-digit Customer ID works on the official portal exactly like the reference number. It is printed on any old bill.

Does the reference number change every month?

No. It stays the same for the same connection, month after month. One old bill is enough to find it forever.

Can someone else's bill help me?

No — each connection has its own reference number. Only a bill for your own meter/connection will show your bill.
